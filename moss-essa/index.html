For your stuffed companions

MOSS ESSA

Little companions. A home for their stories.

A cosy place for your emotional support stuffed animals, whether you have one much-loved companion or a whole little crew.

Coming soonBecome a tester

More than just a name and a photo

ESSA stands for emotional support stuffed animal. MOSS ESSA gives each of your companions a place of their own, with photos, personal details and a diary for the everyday adventures you share. The big outings and the quiet days both get to belong here.

Meet your little crew

Keep your companions in a gallery of photo profiles. Give each one their own details, and keep plush information alongside the things that make them them.

A diary of your days

Write about time spent together and choose which companions were there. Keep the moments you want to remember, in your own words.

Little marks of the day

Add stamps and moods to diary entries to capture what you did and how the day felt. A little detail can bring a whole memory back.

Make yourself at home

Choose a colour theme and journal font to make your little corner feel comfortable. Profiles and diary entries are kept locally on your device.

MOSS ESSA is still being made. You can become a tester if you would like to help try new MOSS apps, or see what else is growing.
