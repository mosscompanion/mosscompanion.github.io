A home for your collections

MOSS Archives

Your own little room of collections.

For favourite finds, carefully kept treasures and the bits you definitely put somewhere safe.

Coming soonPrivacy policy

All your things, with a place to belong

Make a collection, give it a box and fill it with item cards. Keep photos, notes and the details you care about together, so remembering what you have feels a little less like rummaging and a little more like visiting your own cosy archive room.

A box for every collection

Group your things in collections and choose box styles and labels to make your shelves feel like yours.

The details that matter

Give each item a card with photos, notes, dates, prices and custom fields. Keep the information that makes sense for your collection.

Keep a copy handy

Save a backup of your archives and photos, or export your collection details to a spreadsheet when you want another way to look at them.

Your shelves, your way

There is no account to create or MOSS cloud sync. Your collections live on your device, with backups you can choose to save. The privacy policy explains how photos, exports and Android backups work.

MOSS Archives is still being made. For now, you can become a tester if you would like to help try new MOSS apps.
