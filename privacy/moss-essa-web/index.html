MOSS ESSA Web App storage and privacy

Updated 24 November 2026. This notice covers MOSS ESSA Web App at madebymoss.co.uk/essa-app/. The Android app has its own privacy policy.

Your Google account

Google sign-in confirms your Google account identity and email address. The browser uses that identity to open the correct journal and displays your email address so you can see which account is in use. Google also asks for permission to save MOSS ESSA files in its application-data area of your Google Drive.

The browser keeps the Google access token in memory for the current tab. When Google access expires, use Reconnect Google in the account menu. Signing out removes the token from this app's memory. Your Google password is handled by Google.

Your journal and photographs

ESSA profiles, diary entries, photographs, your profile, saved cards and tags, and settings are stored in your own Google Drive. They use your Google storage allowance. MOSS ESSA saves them in a hidden application-data area, accessible through your permission to the app. Other MOSS ESSA users cannot open your files through their accounts.

The website sends journal files directly from your browser to Google Drive. Made by MOSS runs the app as static website files and does not operate a central journal database. Google's handling of account and Drive information is covered by Google's privacy policy.

The copy in this browser

Remember this device is available on the sign-in screen and under Settings → Google Drive & backups. When enabled, it stores your Google account ID and email address locally under moss.essa.rememberedAccount.v1, so the web app can reopen your saved data without signing in again. It does not store your Google password or access token. Anyone using the same browser profile can open that saved data, so use this option on your own device. Turning it off or signing out removes the remembered account, while keeping the local copy of your web app data.

The remembered web app opens from this device without checking Google Drive. Reconnect Google to check for changes from other devices and resume Drive saves. Google connection status is shown under Settings → Google Drive & backups. The web app checks saved versions when Google reconnects.

This browser also stores a local copy for each Google account. Changes save locally as you edit, and the app checks for changes to upload to Drive about every 20 seconds while the journal is open. Settings → Google Drive & backups shows the account and save status. Its three-dot menu has Save / check Google Drive, reconnect, backup and sign-out controls. Wait for the saved message before closing a tab after editing. An offline connection, full Drive, expired permission or another error can delay a Drive save.

Signing out leaves that account's local copy in the browser for later use. Clearing this website's browser data removes local copies and can also remove other Made by MOSS website preferences. Google Drive copies are separate. On a shared computer, use a separate browser profile or private browsing, save to Drive, and close the private window when finished.

Versions, recovery and deletion

The web app keeps previous Drive versions to preserve work if saving is interrupted or two devices edit at the same time. Previous versions can include information you later change or delete in the journal. They continue to use Google storage until removed. A conflict asks which version you want to continue with and preserves the existing Drive versions.

You can use Download backup in the account menu to save a complete .mossbackup file, then reopen it using Restore backup. Earlier JSON device copies are also accepted. These files contain journal content and photos, so keep them somewhere private. Opening a downloaded copy changes the local journal and then saves it to your Drive.

You can remove the application's hidden data through Google Drive's Manage apps settings. Because the web app and Android backup use the same Google Cloud project, removing all MOSS ESSA hidden app data can also remove associated Android backups. Download anything you want to keep first. Revoking Google's access permission prevents subsequent Drive access but does not itself remove browser copies or downloaded files.

Web app and Android

MOSS ESSA Web App starts a separate journal in your Google Drive. The web app's files have their own format and are separate from Android backup files.

Optional web analytics

Google Analytics is off by default. Before it loads, this browser asks whether you want to allow optional visit statistics. Choosing No thanks, or closing the question, leaves analytics off. The journal works the same whether you accept or decline. Your choice is saved locally in this browser under moss_essa_optional_analytics_v1; it is not linked to your Google account.

If you accept, Google Analytics receives visit and device information, such as visits to the web app, browser and device characteristics, approximate location and connection information. It uses analytics cookies, including _ga and a cookie for this property's measurement ID. Google receives ordinary network information when its script loads. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled.

MOSS does not send journal text, ESSA names, photographs, email addresses, sign-in tokens or Google Drive files to Analytics. The page title and page URL sent to Analytics are fixed to MOSS ESSA Web App and https://madebymoss.co.uk/essa-app/, without query strings, fragments or journal-specific routes. No journal-content events are added. These statistics count consenting visitors, rather than every person using the app.

You can change your choice through Settings → Google Drive & backups → Web analytics privacy, or open analytics privacy choices here. Choosing No thanks turns off future Analytics collection in open tabs and removes this app's analytics cookies without deleting journal or sign-in data. Withdrawing consent does not itself delete statistics previously received by Google. Clearing browser preferences asks the question again. This optional setting applies to the web app; it does not enable Analytics in the Android app.

See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services.

Website connections and contact

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Your browser sends ordinary connection information to GitHub to load the app, and to Google to use sign-in and Drive. The app bundles its journal fonts and drawing engine. This information page uses the website's styling, which may load Google Fonts. See GitHub's privacy statement.

For questions, contact hello@madebymoss.co.uk. An email contains whatever information you choose to send. Made by MOSS cannot retrieve your private Drive journal through this website.
