MOSS ESSA web journal storage and privacy

Updated 8 November 2026. This notice covers the browser trial at madebymoss.co.uk/essa-app/. The Android app has its own privacy policy.

Your Google account

Google sign-in confirms your Google account identity and email address. The browser uses that identity to open the correct journal and displays your email address so you can see which account is in use. Google also asks for permission to save MOSS ESSA files in its application-data area of your Google Drive.

The browser keeps the Google access token in memory for the current tab. When Google access expires, use Reconnect Google in the account menu. Signing out removes the token from this app's memory. Your Google password is handled by Google.

Your journal and photographs

ESSA profiles, diary entries, photographs, your profile, saved cards and tags, and settings are stored in your own Google Drive. They use your Google storage allowance. MOSS ESSA saves them in a hidden application-data area, accessible through your permission to the app. Other MOSS ESSA users cannot open your files through their accounts.

The website sends journal files directly from your browser to Google Drive. Made by MOSS runs the app as static website files and does not operate a central journal database. Google's handling of account and Drive information is covered by Google's privacy policy.

The copy in this browser

This browser also stores a local copy for each Google account. Changes save locally as you edit, and the app checks for changes to upload to Drive about every 20 seconds while the journal is open. The account menu has Save / check Google Drive and shows the save status. Wait for the saved message before closing a tab after editing. An offline connection, full Drive, expired permission or another error can delay a Drive save.

Signing out leaves that account's local copy in the browser for later use. Clearing this website's browser data removes local copies and can also remove other Made by MOSS website preferences. Google Drive copies are separate. On a shared computer, use a separate browser profile or private browsing, save to Drive, and close the private window when finished.

Versions, recovery and deletion

The trial keeps previous Drive versions to preserve work if saving is interrupted or two devices edit at the same time. Previous versions can include information you later change or delete in the journal. They continue to use Google storage until removed. A conflict asks which version you want to continue with and preserves the existing Drive versions.

You can use Download journal backup in the account menu to save a complete .mossbackup file, then reopen it using Restore journal backup. Earlier JSON device copies are also accepted. These files contain journal content and photos, so keep them somewhere private. Opening a downloaded copy changes the local journal and then saves it to your Drive.

You can remove the application's hidden data through Google Drive's Manage apps settings. Because the web trial and Android backup use the same Google Cloud project, removing all MOSS ESSA hidden app data can also remove associated Android backups. Download anything you want to keep first. Revoking Google's access permission prevents subsequent Drive access but does not itself remove browser copies or downloaded files.

Web trial and Android

This trial starts a separate web journal in your Google Drive. The trial's files have their own format and are separate from Android backup files.

Website connections and contact

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Your browser sends ordinary connection information to GitHub to load the app, and to Google to use sign-in and Drive. The app bundles its journal fonts and drawing engine. This information page uses the website's styling, which may load Google Fonts. See GitHub's privacy statement.

For questions, contact hello@madebymoss.co.uk. An email contains whatever information you choose to send. Made by MOSS cannot retrieve your private Drive journal through this website.
