Things worth waiting for

MOSS Countdown lets you make and customise countdowns, then put them where they’re most useful: right on your Android home screen.

It grew out of a side quest while Companion was being made. This happens a lot around here.

Make it yours

Name your countdown, choose its look and make the thing you’re waiting for feel visible.

Keep it handy

Home-screen widgets mean the countdown is there at a glance.

Private by default

Your countdown content and chosen images stay locally on your device.

The original privacy-policy URL is preserved. It remains hosted in its existing GitHub Pages repository so links already used by Google Play continue to work.
