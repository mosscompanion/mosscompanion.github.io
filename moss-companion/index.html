A visual aid for the day

MOSS Companion started as something Katie was building for her offspring, who struggles with time, transitions and the general nonsense of being expected to know what “in a bit” means.

It needed to be low demand, so Companion is basically a visual aid for the day. A cute one.

See the day

Visual schedules and a 24-hour clock help turn an abstract day into something you can actually look at.

Now, next, then

Simple boards show what is happening without judging whether anything is “done”.

Widget-minded

Useful information can live on the home screen, without needing to open the app every time.
