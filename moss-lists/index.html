A home for all those things

Keep everything you need to remember in one cosy little library. Create books for different parts of your life, then fill their pages with checklists, notes, dates and all those things you definitely were going to remember. Simple, visual and flexible — organise things in whatever way makes sense to you.

Books for your life

Create a book for whatever needs a home. Rename, recolour and arrange your books to make your library feel like yours.

A page for every list

Fill each book with multiple pages. Choose checklists, notes, bullet lists or date and deadline lists.

Room to do it your way

Name, colour and organise your pages in whatever way makes sense to you. Keep the little things and the big things together.

Simple to settle into

No account to create, no ads and no subscriptions. Your library is stored in the app on your device, with no MOSS cloud sync or shared libraries.

You can choose to export a backup. Android may also back up or transfer app data through your device’s settings. The MOSS Lists privacy policy explains how those work.
