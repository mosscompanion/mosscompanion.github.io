Web analytics privacy

Visit statistics are optional. Refusing or withdrawing permission does not affect your journal. This choice is saved only in this browser.

Analytics counts visits to the web app. It does not receive journal entries, names, photos, email addresses, Google sign-in tokens or Drive files. Google receives ordinary visit and device information if you opt in. Advertising features are disabled.

Choose No thanks to turn analytics off and remove this app’s analytics cookies. Your saved journal and sign-in storage stay intact.
