Become a MOSS tester

A small group of helpful humans who get to try things before everyone else.

What it means

MOSS testers may be invited to try an app before it is publicly released. You use it in everyday life, in whatever way makes sense for you, and tell me how it goes.

Early versions can still have bugs, rough edges or unfinished features. Useful feedback and bug reports are very welcome — including a quick note when something is confusing or not doing what you expected.

Requesting to become a tester does not mean there will always be an app ready to test straight away. When new testing opportunities become available, I can get in touch.
