About MOSS

Hi! I'm Katie, the person behind Made by MOSS!

I'm a mum and carer to two autistic children, and we're a pretty creative, neurodivergent household.

I've always loved making things and being creative, particularly when it comes to design. I'm much more of a crafter than an artist, so drawing hasn't traditionally been my creative outlet. But I've really enjoyed exploring it over the past year, and my confidence is definitely growing!

In my previous job, I built databases using Microsoft Access, so finding ways to organise information, solve problems and make things work better isn't entirely new to me!

It started with Companion

MOSS Companion started as something I was making for my offspring, who struggles with time, transitions and the general nonsense of being expected to know what "in a bit" means.

It began as a simple Now/Next/Then board I made using KWGT, but I quickly found myself wanting to do things it couldn't quite manage.

So I decided to turn it into an app!

It needed to be low demand, so Companion is basically a visual aid for the day. A cute one.

Then came the first side quest

While making Companion, I went on a side quest and made MOSS Countdown too.

I love widgets, so both apps have been made with that in mind. I like useful things that can sit quietly on a home screen and do their job without demanding a whole performance from you.

And the second side quest!

Before either of my first apps were out in the world, I was procrastinating hard at home about all the things I had to do and trying to sort and prioritise them.

Instead of writing a list like I normally would, I decided to create MOSS Lists! And honestly, that little library brings me so much joy!

And then came ESSA!

MOSS ESSA grew out of our household's love of Emotional Support Stuffed Animals.

What started as a way to give our plush companions their own little profiles has grown into somewhere to keep memories, write diaries, create ID badges and tags, make mood boards, organise collections and share wishlists.

It's a bit different from my other apps, but it's been so much fun to make!

How I make my apps

Although I've always enjoyed designing and creating things, programming isn't a skill I've mastered myself.

I use OpenAI Codex to handle the coding for my apps, which has made it possible for me to turn my ideas into something real!

The ideas, designs and artwork are my own. I draw everything myself in ibisPaint, with some creative contributions from my feral offspring, and spend countless hours planning features, testing, tweaking and improving things.

It's been a huge learning experience, and I'm constantly discovering new things I want to try.

Made with love

MOSS is very much a personal passion project. I'm just one person making things I enjoy, often inspired by things that would be useful to our own household.

I love hearing from people who use my apps, and I'm always happy to hear ideas, suggestions and feedback.

I'm really proud of what I've managed to create so far, and I'm excited to see where the next side quest takes me!
