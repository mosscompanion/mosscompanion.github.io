MOSS Countdown

Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Overview

MOSS Countdown is an independent app by Katie at Made by MOSS. Countdowns, count-ups and their designs work locally without an account. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking services, and we do not sell personal information.

Information you create

The app stores event names, dates and times, count directions, wording, widget layouts, colours, fonts, image and sticker edits, and app preferences in its private storage on your device. We do not receive this content on a MOSS server.

Photos and images

You choose images through Android’s image picker. MOSS keeps private copies and originals needed for your designs and stickers. Image editing and automatic background removal take place on your device. Images are sent to Google Drive only if you choose to use Drive backup.

Optional Google Drive backup

In Backup you can connect a Google account and explicitly authorize access to the app’s own private application-data folder using the drive.appdata permission. This permission does not give MOSS access to your other Drive files. Google Sign-In and Google Drive process the connection under Google’s privacy policy and terms. The app keeps your connected email address and backup status locally. Authorization tokens are managed by Google’s sign-in services and are not included in backup files or sent to MOSS.

Backups are uploaded directly from your device to your chosen Google account over HTTPS. They include your saved countdowns and count-ups, designs, wording, preferences, custom images, sticker originals and editing information. MOSS does not operate a server that receives or stores these backups. Backups use space in your Google account and are private app data rather than ordinary files visible in Drive browsing.

Back up now uploads a saved snapshot when you request it. Automatic backups are off by default. If you enable them, saved changes are queued after a quiet period; Android controls when background work can run. Wi-Fi only is enabled by default and can be changed in Backup. No upload is required to use the app.

Restore, disconnection and deletion

Restoring requires confirmation before replacing local countdowns, designs and settings. Automatic backups are switched off after a Drive restore so you can review your restored data. Android home-screen widgets may need adding again.

Disconnect Google account stops automatic backups and signs the app out on this device. Disconnection, clearing local app data and uninstalling do not delete existing Google Drive backups. You can revoke MOSS’s access in your Google Account’s connected-app controls and remove its hidden backup data using Google Drive’s app-management settings. MOSS does not automatically delete older remote backup versions. Your local data remains until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall.

Local backup files

You can export a local ZIP backup to Downloads/MOSS Countdown and restore it later without Google. The file contains saved countdowns, preferences and images. Anyone with access to the file may be able to read it, so keep it somewhere private. Local exported copies remain until you delete them yourself.

Notifications, timers and widgets

If you grant notification permission, MOSS can notify you when a countdown finishes. Android alarms and widget services refresh countdown and elapsed-time displays. Android may defer updates or background backups because of battery, storage or network restrictions. You can change notification permission in Android settings.

Data sharing and security

We do not collect countdown content, photos, contacts, location, advertising identifiers or usage activity for advertising or analytics. Optional Drive backup sends the information described above to the Google account you select. Android, Google Play and Google account services may process their own service and diagnostic information under Google’s policies. Google account permissions do not create a separate MOSS online account.

Android protects app-private storage, and Drive requests use encrypted HTTPS connections. Backups are not separately encrypted with a password by MOSS. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Support and contact

Report a bug and Send feedback open your email app. Messages and any details you choose to include are sent only when you send the email and are used to respond to your request. Please avoid including private information you do not want to share. For privacy questions or requests about support correspondence, contact hello@madebymoss.co.uk.

Children’s privacy

The same local storage and optional-backup choices apply to everyone using MOSS. We do not knowingly collect children’s information for advertising, tracking or profiling.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features or requirements change. The date above identifies the current version.
